## 1. Founding principles: Markkula 1977, Jobs 1997 "marketing is about values", focus/saying no, Sinek's "Start with Why"

### Takeaway
The only documented *Apple-authored* marketing doctrine is Markkula's one-page 1977 memo (Empathy, Focus, Impute) and Jobs's 1997 internal talk introducing "Think Different" (marketing = communicating values, not specs, in a noisy world). Sinek's "golden circle" is an outside interpretation applied after the fact, not an Apple method, and its evidence base is criticised.

### Cited Findings
- Mike Markkula wrote a one-page memo titled "The Apple Marketing Philosophy"; coverage describes it as three paragraphs / about 96 words, and dates it to 3 January 1977. The original artifact is hosted by the Steve Jobs Archive (reliability A for the artifact; I could not open the page this session). — [Steve Jobs Archive](https://stevejobsarchive.com/artifact/the-apple-marketing-philosophy); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/wbna45089030)
- The three principles as reported: (1) **Empathy** – understanding customer needs (better than any other company); (2) **Focus** – to do a good job on what Apple decides to do, it must eliminate all the "unimportant opportunities"; (3) **Impute** – a company and its products are judged by the signals they convey; a creative, professional presentation lets Apple "impute the desired qualities" to its products (i.e. people do judge a book by its cover). (B/C; wording via secondary reproductions.) — [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/wbna45089030); [OfficeChai](https://officechai.com/learn/this-was-apples-marketing-strategy-from-1977/); [AppleInsider, "Apple at 50: Mike Markkula", 13 Mar 2026](https://appleinsider.com/articles/26/03/13/apple-at-50-mike-markkula-apples-second-ceo-was-as-important-as-steve-jobs)
- Jobs's 1997 talk: delivered internally in Cupertino around September 1997 (one source: about eight weeks after his return as interim CEO; audience described variously as "employees" and "senior managers"). Core lines reproduced across sources: marketing is about values; the world is complicated and noisy and a company will not get much chance to be remembered, so it must be very clear what it wants people to know about it. He uses Nike as the model (sells shoes, but the advertising honours great athletes and never talks about the product). He frames "Think Different" as an attempt to get Apple back to its core values. (B/C) — [Presentation Zen](https://presentationzen.com/blog/steve-jobs-on-communicating-your-core-values); [Speakola transcript](https://speakola.com/corp/tag/APPLE); [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/jason-aten/steve-jobs-stole-his-best-idea-ever-from-nikes-brilliant-2-word-marketing-strategy.html); [Fortune, 7 Oct 2015](https://dc.fortune.com/2015/10/07/apple-real-steve-jobs/)
- **Caveat on the 1997 talk:** there is no official Apple transcript; circulating transcripts are community-made from the video, and wording differs between them (e.g. "very noisy world" vs "complicated and noisy world"). Treat any quote as approximate. — [Presentation Zen](https://presentationzen.com/blog/steve-jobs-on-communicating-your-core-values); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/resources/timeless-branding-lessons-from-a-young-steve-jobs)
- Sinek's model is his own framing: Apple succeeds because it "starts with why". Critiques found: (a) his claim that the model is "grounded in the tenets of biology" is judged weak by a genetics/evolutionary-biology academic quoted in one critique; (b) historically Apple started with a product (Apple I), and one critic "couldn't find much evidence of 'think different'" thinking in early-era materials; (c) the model omits "who" (the customer); (d) University of Arkansas Walton College piece: "Start with Why. Just don't end there." All critiques are essays/blogs, not empirical tests (C, one B). — [Medium, "Why Simon Sinek is fundamentally wrong"](https://medium.com/melting-pot/why-simon-sinek-is-fundamentally-wrong-7d743954cb5); [UX Planet](https://uxplanet.org/3-things-wrong-with-simon-sineks-golden-circle-f262fed6ce3f); [Nick Asbury, "The story of why"](https://nickasbury.substack.com/p/the-story-of-why); [Walton College](https://walton.uark.edu/insights/posts/start-with-why-just-dont-end-there.php)

### Inferences
- Markkula's memo and Jobs's 1997 talk say different, compatible things: the memo is about *product and presentation* (understand needs, do few things, make every touchpoint signal quality); the 1997 talk is about *brand advertising* (values over specs). Neither says "lead with purpose instead of product" — Apple's actual launch ads are heavily product-centred. Sinek collapses both into "why", which is a simplification.
- "Impute" is the principle that links advertising to everything else (packaging, website, stores): the ad is only one of the signals; a slipshod touchpoint elsewhere undoes it.
- For a small Mac-app company: Empathy/Focus/Impute is directly usable; "marketing is about values" was said by a company with near-universal awareness and should not be read as "don't show the product".

### Gaps
- Exact memo text and exact 1997 wording not verified against primary artifact/video this session (host blocked).
- No peer-reviewed test of the golden-circle claim about Apple was found; critiques are opinion pieces.
- Jobs's "focus is about saying no" (WWDC 1997) and "innovation is saying no to a thousand things": well-known, but not sourced this session — see Unverified background at the end.

## 2. Organisation and agency model: TBWA\Chiat\Day → Media Arts Lab, in-house Marcom, 2024–2026 changes, how creative is briefed and approved

### Takeaway
Apple runs a hybrid: a dedicated, Apple-only agency (TBWA\Media Arts Lab, MAL) plus a large in-house Marketing Communications group under Tor Myhren (since 2016), who briefs both and whose remit spans advertising, the website and packaging. As of late 2026 the relationship with MAL continues; I found no credible report of an agency review or of Myhren leaving. The big 2026 change is at the top: John Ternus became CEO on 1 Sept 2026 and Myhren now reports to him.

### Cited Findings
- TBWA\Media Arts Lab is owned by TBWA\Chiat\Day and Apple is its only client. (B) — [MacRumors, 5 Nov 2019](https://www.macrumors.com/2019/11/05/media-arts-lab-apple-advertising-employee-cuts/)
- 2014: Bloomberg-sourced reports that Apple began producing TV ads internally while still collaborating with MAL, hired Tyler Whisnand from MAL to lead an internal creative team, and planned an in-house team of up to 1,000; reports in July 2014 that Apple might part ways with TBWA did not materialise. (B, second-hand via MacRumors) — [MacRumors, 4 Jun 2014](https://www.macrumors.com/2014/06/04/apple-tv-ads-internally-still-collaborating/); [MacRumors, 10 Jun 2014](https://www.macrumors.com/2014/06/10/apple-ads-in-house-1000/); [MacRumors, 14 Jul 2014](https://www.macrumors.com/2014/07/14/apple-tbwa-breakup-jimmy-iovine/)
- Tor Myhren (ex-Grey) was named VP Marketing Communications; started 2016. (B) — [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/265065/greys-top-creative-tor-myhren-is-joining-apple.html); [MacRumors, 29 Apr 2016](https://www.macrumors.com/2016/04/29/tor-myhren-vp-marketing-communications/)

- Myhren's remit per Apple: Marketing Communications covers Apple's advertising, internet presence, package design and other consumer-facing marketing; the leadership page (as indexed in 2026) says he reports to CEO John Ternus. (A) — [Apple Leadership: Tor Myhren](https://www.apple.com/leadership/tor-myhren/)
- How work is made today: "Shot on iPhone" is a combination of the in-house team and MAL; Myhren briefs the internal team and MAL separately, "but ultimately everyone works together". (B) — [AppleInsider, 25 Aug 2025](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/08/25/how-steve-jobss-legacy-still-drives-the-shot-on-iphone-ad-agency); background on MAL's evolution: [Adweek, "From Jobs and Clow to Now"](https://www.adweek.com/agencies/apple-media-arts-lab-steve-jobs-lee-clow-evolving-marketing/)
- 2026 Fast Company exclusive on Myhren's ten years: Apple was about a $540bn company when he started in 2016 and about $4.3tn at publication; remit widened with TV, headphones, watch and services; covers what changed and "what didn't work". (B; I could only see the summary.) — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91512780/apple-vp-of-marketing-tor-myhren-10-years)
- 2026 status of MAL: still producing Apple's major work (holiday film "The Dreamer" credited to TBWA\Media Arts Lab; Apple/MAL partnership won a One Show Penta Pencil in 2026); MAL made senior hires in mid-2026 (head of strategy LA; global head of music reporting to global CCO Brent Anderson). (B/C) — [DesignRush](https://news.designrush.com/apple-tbwa-media-arts-lab-holiday-ad-the-dreamer); [LBBOnline](https://lbbonline.com/news/TBWAMedia-Arts-Lab-Anh-Do-Global-Head-of-Music)
- Top-level change: Apple announced on 20 April 2026 that Tim Cook becomes executive chairman and John Ternus CEO effective 1 September 2026; the transition took effect. (A/B) — [Apple Newsroom](https://apple.com/newsroom/2026/04/tim-cook-to-become-apple-executive-chairman-john-ternus-to-become-apple-ceo/); [Al Jazeera, 1 Sep 2026](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/9/1/john-ternus-succeeds-tim-cook-as-apple-ceo-after-15-years)
- Greg Joswiak has been SVP Worldwide Marketing since taking over from Phil Schiller in 2020; no 2026 role change found. (B) — [Fortune, 8 Dec 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/12/08/apple-executive-exodus-2026-new-leadership-team-rising-stars-tim-cook-successor/?rand=8593)
- Ken Segall (agency creative director for Jobs for 12 years across NeXT and Apple; his team named the iMac and worked on "Think different") on approval: other companies get stuck in approval layers, and a better idea arriving late is ignored because expectations are set; Jobs sat in the early meetings himself, so the decision-maker judged ideas directly. (A for Segall's own account; B) — [Information Age interview](https://information-age.com/?p=28313); [HubSpot interview](https://blog.hubspot.com/agency/pov-interview-with-ken-segall); [Wikipedia: Ken Segall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Segall)
- Segall's 2016 view on what an in-house agency needs to succeed. (B/C) — [iPhone in Canada, 4 Jan 2016](https://www.iphoneincanada.ca/2016/01/04/apple-in-house-agency/)
- A recent failure of the approval system: the May 2024 iPad Pro "Crush" film; Myhren told Ad Age "We missed the mark with this video, and we're sorry", and the spot was not run on TV. (B) — [Fortune, 10 May 2024](https://fortune.com/2024/05/10/apple-apology-ipad-crush-advert/); [Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/culture/culture-news/apple-apologizes-crush-ipad-ad-1235018260/)

### Inferences
- The stable features across 30 years: one dedicated creative partner with no competing clients, a short approval chain ending in a senior person who is present early, and one executive owning ads + website + packaging so all touchpoints look and sound alike (Markkula's "Impute" as an org chart).
- Product marketing (Joswiak: positioning, line-up, keynote narrative) and marketing communications (Myhren: execution) are separate lines; positioning precedes creative.
- The task brief's premise of a 2024–2026 "agency review" is **not supported** by anything I found; the evidence points to continuity with MAL.

### Gaps
- No credible source found for a 2024–2026 agency review, a Myhren departure, or a changed Joswiak role. Bloomberg/Ad Age/Adweek paywalled pieces could not be read; absence of evidence here is not proof.
- Size of the in-house team today and the in-house/MAL split by volume: not found.
- Segall's specific rules ("small groups of smart people", no focus groups, the brief on one page): in the book, not sourced this session.

## 3. Launch choreography: secrecy, event films, reveal → pre-order → Friday availability, embargoes, earned media, design/packaging, naming

### Takeaway
Apple launches follow a fixed, repeated calendar: a pre-recorded event film, pre-orders within days, availability on a Friday one to two weeks later, with reviews landing around release. The pattern held unchanged through the September and October 2026 launches. I found no reliable quantification of the earned-media value.

### Cited Findings
- Events are pre-recorded films; press still attend at Apple Park to see products hands-on afterwards (2025 iPhone 17 event). (B) — [AppleInsider, 9 Sep 2025](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/09/09/check-out-the-apple-iphone-17-event-with-appleinsiders-in-person-coverage)
- The full film is published straight to YouTube and Apple's events page (e.g. 2021 "California Streaming"); smaller launches get short films instead of events (Oct 2024: about 10-minute M4 iMac video; 17-minute MacBook Pro "video announcement and ad"). (B) — [MacRumors, 14 Sep 2021](https://www.macrumors.com/2021/09/14/apple-california-streaming-youtube/); [MacRumors, 28 Oct 2024](https://www.macrumors.com/2024/10/28/apple-m4-imac-keynote/); [MacRumors, 30 Oct 2024](https://www.macrumors.com/2024/10/30/apple-shares-macbook-pro-video-announcement/)
- Cadence examples: 2024 – event Mon 9 Sep, pre-orders Fri 13 Sep, availability Fri 20 Sep. 2025 – event Tue 9 Sep, pre-orders that Friday, availability 19 Sep. (B) — [MacRumors, 9 Sep 2024](https://www.macrumors.com/2024/09/09/everything-apple-announced-fall-2024-event/); [AppleInsider, 9 Sep 2025](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/09/09/check-out-the-apple-iphone-17-event-with-appleinsiders-in-person-coverage)
- 2026 (per one tracking blog that says it verified against Apple's pages; C): iPhone 18 Pro/Pro Max pre-orders Sat 12 Sep, availability Fri 18 Sep in 65+ countries; a foldable model with pre-orders Fri 16 Oct; October Mac/iPad launch with same-day pre-orders in 30 countries and availability Fri 6 Nov. — [blakecrosley.com, Sept 2026](https://blakecrosley.com/ja/blog/apple-event-september-2026); [blakecrosley.com, Oct 2026](https://blakecrosley.com/blog/apple-event-october-2026)
- Review embargoes: reviews typically appear on or just before release day (forum observation only; C). — [MacRumors Forums](https://forums.macrumors.com/threads/curious-lack-of-reviews-on-the-m5-pro-and-m5-max-macbook-pros.2478728/)
- Packaging sits inside Marcom (same executive as advertising), i.e. is managed as marketing. (A) — [Apple Leadership: Tor Myhren](https://www.apple.com/leadership/tor-myhren/)
- Naming is treated as marketing craft: Segall's agency team named the iMac. (B) — [Wikipedia: Ken Segall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Segall); [The Next Web excerpt of Insanely Simple](https://thenextweb.com/news/read-an-excerpt-of-insanely-simple-a-great-new-book-about-steve-jobs-and-apple-by-ken-segall)

### Inferences
- The sequence compresses attention: the gap between "first saw it" and "can order it" is days, so the ad/film converts while interest is at its peak. The film itself is the first and longest ad; the 30-second spots are cut-downs of a story already told.
- Predictability is an asset: because the calendar repeats every year, press, reviewers and customers plan around it, which produces coverage without paid media.
- The one time Apple broke "announce only what ships" (Apple Intelligence, section 8) is the one launch that ended in a regulator finding and a settlement.

### Gaps
- No credible figure for the earned-media value of an Apple launch found.
- "Pre-recorded since 2020 (WWDC, COVID)" is correct to my knowledge but I only have sources showing the format from 2021 on.
- Secrecy practices (Lashinsky's "Inside Apple") and unboxing/packaging design detail: not sourced this session.

## 4. Media strategy and spend

### Takeaway
Apple last disclosed advertising expense for fiscal 2015 ($1.8bn, a record, up 50%); since the FY2016 10-K it is folded into SG&A. Third-party trackers only see slices. The best-evidenced channel fact is that Apple was the number-one US out-of-home advertiser in 2025 — consistent with "Shot on iPhone" billboards being its signature medium.

### Cited Findings
- Apple reported a record $1.8bn advertising expense for FY2015 (up 50%); the FY2016 10-K (filed Oct 2016) dropped the line and gave no reason. A Wells Fargo note speculated about motives; that is speculation. (A for the filing, B for coverage) — [SEC: Apple FY2015 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000119312515356351/d17062d10k.htm); [WARC](https://www.warc.com/newsandopinion/news/apple-no-longer-discloses-adspend/en-gb/37819); [9to5Mac, 24 Nov 2016](https://9to5mac.com/2016/11/24/apple-aapl-ad-spend-2016/)
- Out-of-home: Apple led the OAAA list of top US OOH advertisers for 2025 (ahead of Morgan & Morgan and Vivint in one write-up; another headline lists Apple, Verizon, Coca-Cola). (B) — [Digital Signage Today](https://www.digitalsignagetoday.com/news/apple-morgan-morgan-vivint-lead-top-ooh-advertisers-list/); [Billboard Insider](https://billboardinsider.com/?p=137807)
- MediaRadar's advertiser profile estimates Apple at "over $100 million" on digital, print and national TV in the trailing year, down year over year. This is a narrow panel estimate (no OOH, search, cinema, most social, non-US) and clearly understates total spend. (B, low precision) — [MediaRadar](https://advertisers.mediaradar.com/apple-advertising-profile)
- Do not confuse with Apple's *ad revenue* (Search Ads etc.): eMarketer put Apple's US ad business at $6.47bn (2024) and projected $7.42bn (2025). (B) — [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/apple-ad-business-quietly-contributes-record-services-revenues)
- "Shot on iPhone": launched March 2015 as "World Gallery" for iPhone 6; reported scope 77 photographers, 70 cities, 24 countries, and (another source) 10,000+ billboard/poster sites; counts vary. Myhren called it "a ridiculously simple idea based on behavior we were seeing with people posting their photos and hashtagging them". Billboards could be local (a photo taken in a city shown in that city). Campaign turned ten in 2025 and won a major industry award. (B) — [MacRumors, 2 Mar 2015](https://www.macrumors.com/2015/03/02/SHOT-ON-IPHONE-6-AD-CAMPAIGN/); [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/how-shot-on-iphone-became-a-thing_id136380); [TIME](https://time.com/3726913/apple-iphone-photo-campaign); [MacRumors, 19 Jun 2025](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/06/19/apples-shot-on-iphone-campaign-wins-award/)
- Caveats on "Shot on iPhone": sales uplift claims coincide with the iPhone 6s launch and are not isolated; "user-generated" understates the curation and professional production involved. (C) — [The Brand Hopper](https://thebrandhopper.com/campaigns/a-case-study-on-apples-shot-on-iphone-brand-campaign/)
- Sports/TV placement example: the 2019 privacy spot ran through March Madness in the US before going to other markets. (B) — [MacRumors, 14 Mar 2019](https://www.macrumors.com/2019/03/14/apple-privacy-matters-iphone-ad/)

### Inferences
- Apple buys few, large, premium placements (big outdoor, live sport, its own YouTube/site) where the creative can be one image and a few words; this suits a brand whose ad format is "product or result + white space + logo".
- Because the creative shows the *output* of the product (a photo) rather than a claim, the medium is the proof — a pattern a software company can copy by showing real output.

### Gaps
- No reliable total ad-spend estimate after FY2015; no Kantar/Vivvix/Sensor Tower/Ad Age figures retrieved (paywalled or not surfaced). Share-of-voice data: none found.
- Brand-asset consistency (San Francisco typeface, white space, sonic assets) and "no discounts/performance-style ads": widely observed but not sourced this session.

## 5. From ad to purchase: website, retail, pricing architecture, trade-in, loyalty

### Takeaway
Apple owns the conversion points its ads lead to (apple.com and its own stores), prices in a good-better-best ladder that steers buyers to the middle or up, and retains about nine in ten US iPhone owners. Hard numbers exist for retail productivity (historic) and loyalty (current CIRP); pricing-psychology claims are analyst interpretation.

### Cited Findings
- Retail productivity: around 2009–2011 Apple Stores did roughly $4,000–4,300 in sales per square foot versus about $872 for Best Buy; reported as more than Tiffany. Dated figures. (B) — [Fortune, 26 Aug 2011](https://fortune.com/2011/08/26/how-apple-became-the-best-retailer-in-america/); [Signal v. Noise](https://signalvnoise.com/posts/304-apple-stores-get-the-last-laugh); [Fortune, 30 Oct 2010](https://fortune.com/2010/10/30/44-million-for-mr-genius-bar)
- Ron Johnson (hired from Target) pushed the Genius Bar over Jobs's initial objection — "instead of dispensing alcohol, we dispense advice" — after a group asked for their best service experience mostly named hotels (16 of 18). Stores were prototyped in a warehouse on Mickey Drexler's advice. (B) — [Fortune, 26 Aug 2011](https://fortune.com/2011/08/26/how-apple-became-the-best-retailer-in-america/); [9to5Mac feature](https://9to5mac.com/67978/feature-retail-stores-apples-big-gamble-that-paid-off-big-time/)
- Pricing ladder: analysts describe the line-up as good-better-best; "never underestimate the popularity of Better" (Asymco); buyers pick the middle to avoid losing features or overpaying (Wiglaf Journal on iPhone 12); S&P Global: the pricing structure nudges consumers toward Pro models; low base storage pushes buyers up a tier. Stratechery: XS/XR strategy and pricing were planned two to three years ahead. (B, interpretation) — [Asymco, 20 Sep 2017](https://www.asymco.com/2017/09/20/good-better-best); [Wiglaf Journal](https://wiglafjournal.com/iphone-12-strategy-review/); [S&P Global Market Intelligence](https://www.spglobal.com/marketintelligence/en/news-insights/blog/apples-latest-iphone-pricing-structure-nudges-consumers-toward-pro-models); [Stratechery, "The iPhone Franchise"](https://stratechery.com/?p=3679)
- 2025 example of deliberate price overlap: iPhone Air 256 GB and iPhone 17 512 GB at the same price. (C) — [IDN Times](https://www.idntimes.com/tech/gadget/kenapa-harga-iphone-air-256-gb-dan-iphone-17-512-gb-persis-c1c2-01-7cxy7-bcsdg2)
- Loyalty (CIRP, US): iPhone loyalty 87% in Q1 2026, up from 84% (Q1 2025) and 85% (Q1 2024); only 12% of new iPhone buyers in the March 2026 quarter came from Android. For the 12 months to June 2025 CIRP reported 89% overall, 92% when the buyer keeps their carrier and 79% when switching carrier. CIRP does not publish sample size or margin of error. (B) — [MacRumors, 17 Jul 2026](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/07/17/iphone-loyalty-rate-climbs-to-87/); [AppleInsider, 27 Aug 2025](https://appleinsider.com/articles/25/08/27/apple-loyalty-softens-with-carrier-switches-but-still-outpaces-android); [CIRP Substack](https://cirpapple.substack.com/p/did-iphone-loyalty-reach-its-limit)
- SellCell stated-intent survey: iPhone owners intending to stay rose from 90.5% (2019) to 96.4% (2026); Android 86.4%. Stated intent from a reseller's survey; weaker than CIRP. (C) — [PhoneArena](https://www.phonearena.com/news/iphone-loyalty-rises-in-2026_id181884)

### Inferences
- With roughly 87–89% retention and only about 12% of buyers coming from Android, most iPhone advertising in the US is in effect *upgrade and reassurance* advertising to existing customers; the ad's job is "reason to upgrade now", and the installed base does the rest.
- Loyalty is partly a channel effect (carrier moments), not only brand love — a reminder that conversion depends on the purchase context, not the ad alone.
- Owning the destination (site + store) means the ad's promise, the product page and the sales conversation use the same words and images.

### Gaps
- Current sales-per-square-foot, ACSI score, NPS, Today at Apple/Ahrendts material, trade-in and instalment messaging, apple.com copy/scroll design analysis, decoy-pricing evidence: nothing reliable retrieved this session.

## 6. Consistency over time and what Apple does not do

### Takeaway
Apple's main campaign platforms run for many years with the same line and format: "Shot on iPhone" since March 2015 (11+ years) and "Privacy. That's iPhone" since March 2019 (7+ years).

### Cited Findings
- "Shot on iPhone" launched March 2015; relaunched for iPhone 6s in Feb 2016, extended (e.g. "Colors", June 2016), public "challenge" from 2019, award at its ten-year mark in 2025. (B) — [MacRumors, 2 Mar 2015](https://www.macrumors.com/2015/03/02/SHOT-ON-IPHONE-6-AD-CAMPAIGN/); [MacRumors, 1 Feb 2016](https://www.macrumors.com/2016/02/01/apple-relaunches-shot-on-iphone-6s/); [MacRumors, 28 Jun 2016](https://www.macrumors.com/2016/06/28/apple-shot-on-iphone-colors-campaign/); [MacRumors, 19 Jun 2025](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/06/19/apples-shot-on-iphone-campaign-wins-award/)
- Privacy: CES January 2019 billboard "What happens on your iPhone, stays on your iPhone", then the March 2019 film ending "Privacy. That's iPhone." (B) — [9to5Mac, 14 Mar 2019](https://9to5mac.com/2019/03/14/iphone-privacy-ad/); [MacRumors, 14 Mar 2019](https://www.macrumors.com/2019/03/14/apple-privacy-matters-iphone-ad/)
- Counter-view: Proton (a competitor in privacy products, so interested party) argues the privacy claim overstates reality because Apple gathers data via its services. (C, biased) — [Proton blog](https://proton.me/blog/iphone-privacy)
- Style shift noted around 2015–16: from largely product-oriented ads toward more "lifestyle" branding. (B) — [AppleInsider, 17 Dec 2015](https://appleinsider.com/articles/15/12/17/as-apples-new-marketing-vp-tor-myhren-rides-in-on-a-wave-of-ad-industry-success)

### Inferences
- Each long-running platform is one product benefit stated in two to four words and proven by demonstration; the yearly work is new executions, not new ideas.
- A long-running claim invites scrutiny (Proton); it has to be true at product level to last.

### Gaps
- Apple's tone-of-voice/style guidelines and the list of "never do" rules (no spec comparison, no price in brand ads): no primary source found this session. Note that Apple *has* done comparative advertising ("Get a Mac", 2006–09; the 2025 Underdogs film mocking the Windows blue screen), so "Apple never compares" is a myth as stated.

## 8. Marketing software/AI: Apple Intelligence 2024–2025

### Takeaway
Apple advertised Siri/Apple Intelligence capabilities for iPhone 16 that had not shipped. Result: an NAD recommendation to change or stop claims, a pulled ad, consolidated class actions, and a $250m settlement in May 2026 (no admission of wrongdoing). It is the clearest documented case of Apple's system failing — and it failed exactly where it departed from "advertise what exists".

### Cited Findings
- NAD (BBB National Programs' National Advertising Division) reviewed the claims in early 2025 and recommended Apple modify or discontinue several; it found the "Available Now" banner with footnote disclosures did not adequately qualify the features shown. Apple took the challenged demo video down. NAD decisions are voluntary and non-binding, but plaintiffs quoted them in the amended complaint. (B, law-firm analysis) — [Loeb & Loeb, May 2026](https://www.loeb.com/en/insights/passle/2026/05/when-ai-marketing-gets-ahead-of-ai-engineering)
- A proposed class action was filed in federal court in San Jose in March 2025, highlighting the September 2024 ad with Bella Ramsey showing Siri capabilities that were then delayed; more suits followed in the US and Canada and were consolidated; Apple moved to dismiss in September 2025. (B) — [Axios, 20 Mar 2025](https://www.axios.com/2025/03/20/apple-suit-false-advertising-ai-intelligence); [MacRumors, 10 Apr 2025](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/04/10/apple-hit-with-more-siri-delay-lawsuits/); [MacRumors, 26 Sep 2025](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/09/26/apple-responds-to-delayed-siri-features-lawsuit/); [Truth in Advertising](https://truthinadvertising.org/articles/apple-intelligence-lawsuits-mounting/)
- May 2026: Apple agreed to pay $250 million to settle the consumer class action over how it marketed Apple Intelligence and upgraded Siri for iPhone 16; claims were not proven in court and Apple admitted no wrongdoing. Reported as a proposed settlement; final approval status not confirmed. (B) — [MacRumors, 5 May 2026](https://macrumors.com/2026/05/05/apple-class-action-siri-lawsuit-settlement/); [ACS Information Age](https://ia.acs.org.au/article/2026/apple-to-pay-iphone-users-over-delayed-siri-ai.html)
- Related creative misstep in the same period: iPad Pro "Crush" (May 2024), apology and TV run cancelled. (B) — [Fortune, 10 May 2024](https://fortune.com/2024/05/10/apple-apology-ipad-crush-advert/)

### Inferences
- Lessons that the facts support: (1) advertise only shipped functionality; (2) small-print qualifiers do not rescue a headline claim like "available now"; (3) a filmed demo of a feature is itself a claim; (4) even a non-binding self-regulatory finding becomes evidence in court; (5) the brand's long record of delivering what it showed is what made the breach costly.

### Gaps
- Per-claimant payout, exact settlement terms, and final court approval: not confirmed. NAD decision date and exact claim list: seen only via law-firm summary.
